02 · How I Got Here

From transportation engineering to machine learning and computer science.

My background began in civil and transportation engineering, where I became interested in traffic operations and intelligent infrastructure. That interest eventually led me toward machine learning and computer science as tools for studying control, coordination, and resilience in transportation systems.

Today, my research sits at that intersection. Outside of research, I enjoy baseball and small technical projects that let me experiment with data, modeling, and software in a more personal setting.
